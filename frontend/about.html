College project

MargRakshak AI

Web-based intelligent driver fatigue and distraction detection. A supervisor opens this site, watches a live session, and gets alerts when the driver looks drowsy, yawns, or looks away. Phone detection with YOLO is planned for a later phase.

Phase 1 — now

Frontend screens with dummy data: landing, login, dashboard, alerts, analytics, settings. No camera. No Flask.

Later phases

Flask API, SQLite, webcam, MediaPipe landmarks, rule-based EAR/MAR/head-pose, then YOLO for phone use.

How detection will work (not coded yet)

  1. Camera frames are captured in the browser or on the Flask server.
  2. MediaPipe finds face landmarks (eyes, mouth, head).
  3. Simple rules: if eyes stay closed (low EAR), yawn (high MAR), or head drops, raise an alert.
  4. YOLO later looks for a phone in the frame.
  5. Events are stored in SQLite and shown on these same pages.
